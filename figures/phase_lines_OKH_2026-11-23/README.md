# Phase-line update: OKH 22 III 41 lines (2026-11-23)

From 2026-11-20 the phase lines are the four OKH "Revision of Tasks" 22 III 41 lines, digitized from the slide
"AGS DD Planning Map 15II41" (`milestones/build_okh_lines.py`). They replace the historian's lines a–e. This folder is
a snapshot of every figure and table that depends on the phase lines, regenerated on the new lines, to hand to the authors.
The originals live in the analysis folders and are regenerated by the scripts listed below, so re-copy after a rerun.

The historian-line versions of everything here are kept for comparison: `analysis/*_ags/` and `figures/arrival/*_ags.*`.

## Method changes that came with the new lines
- **Natural breaks** (`jenks_by_phase`): each phase is measured at right angles to its own line (bearing beside each
  strip: about 116–143° for lines 1–3, 195° for line 4), not along the whole advance's 101°. The 4th line runs
  west–east, and the divisions crossed it moving south.
- **Front profiles and shifts** (`front_profile`, `places_in_line`, stretch statistics): position along the front is km
  across the 101° advance (SSW to NNE), not north–south km.
- **Past the 4th line** means south of it and east of its west end (Vinnytsia). Divisions coming from the west, south of
  the line, count as crossing it at the Vinnytsia meridian. This is the same "extend the ends straight" rule the other lines use.
- **Placeholder dates:** the OKH lines have no dates on the slide, so lines 1–4 reuse the agreed phase 1–4 dates
  (30 Jun, 7 Jul, 22 Jul, 25 Jul). **These need the author's confirmation.**
